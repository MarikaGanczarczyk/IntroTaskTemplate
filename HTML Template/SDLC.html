Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)

The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a structured process used for developing software applications. Explain the stages of the SDLC below, in detail:

Planning

What happens:

The team identifies the main goals of the project, the problem it needs to solve, risks, timeline, and resources.

Inputs

  • Business case
  • Initial ideas
  • High-level project goals

Outputs

  • Project plan / schedule
  • Timeline
  • Feasibility analysis

Who is involved ?

  • Project Manager
  • Product Owner
  • Stakeholders
  • Senior Developers

Requirements

What happens:

The team gathers detailed requirements from the business and end users. They describe what the system must do.

Inputs

  • User stories
  • Business requirements
  • Interviews with users

Outputs

  • Requirements document
  • Acceptance criteria
  • User stories ready for development

Who is involved ?

  • Business Analyst
  • Product Owner
  • Users
  • Developers

Design

What happens:

The system architecture, database structure, and user interface are designed.

Inputs

  • Requirements
  • Technical constraints

Outputs

  • Wireframes
  • System architecture
  • Database schema

Who is involved ?

  • Business Analyst
  • UX/UI Designer
  • Developers
