Teams/Slack

I communicate with my colleagues mainly using Microsoft Teams (and Slack when required). I use instant messaging platforms for quick questions, short updates, or clarifications. This is the best option when I need a fast response or want to check something without interrupting someone with a meeting. It is also useful for day-to-day collaboration and informal team communication.

Email

I use email for more formal communication within the company. This is especially important when sending detailed information, documentation, or confirmations that need a clear record or paper trail. Email is useful when the information is not urgent and needs to be stored or referenced later.

Video Call

I set up video calls when a topic needs more discussion or when I expect a lot of back-and-forth questions. This helps avoid misunderstandings and allows everyone to explain their ideas clearly. Video calls are also useful for problem-solving sessions, feedback discussions, or team meetings where interaction is important.

Face-to-Face

I choose face-to-face meetings for more formal situations or when working closely with multiple people at the same time. This is the most effective way to collaborate, share ideas, and build stronger working relationships. In-person communication helps improve understanding and engagement, especially during workshops or planning sessions.
